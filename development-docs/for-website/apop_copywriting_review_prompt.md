# APoP Website Copywriting Review Prompt

**Purpose**: Review and rewrite website copy to position APoP (Agent Policy Protocol) as a **progressive, enabling** technology—not a regressive, restrictive one.

---

## 🎯 Core Positioning Strategy

### The Narrative:

APoP is the **authorization layer that enables the agentic web**, not a barrier to it. It provides clarity and legal confidence that allows AI agents and websites to innovate together.

### The Hero:

AI agents + website owners working together with clear, transparent rules

### The Villain:

Legal uncertainty, blanket blocking, and the fragmented status quo (exemplified by Amazon-Perplexity conflict)

### The Solution:

APoP creates mutual clarity—like robots.txt enabled the search engine era, APoP enables the agentic era.

---

**Enabling & Innovation**:

- "Enables safe AI agent innovation"
- "Making the agentic web possible"
- "Enabling the next generation of agentic applications"
- "Agents can innovate confidently"
- "Reduces friction for agent developers"

- "Legal clarity reduces friction"

- "Both sides win"

- "Stops web fragmentation"

---

## ❌ BAD FRAMING (Avoid or Rewrite)

### Language Patterns to ELIMINATE:

**Control & Restriction** (sounds anti-agent):

- ❌ "Control AI agents"
- ❌ "Restrict agent access"
- ❌ "Manage agent behavior"
- ❌ "Limit what agents can do"
- ✅ **Instead say**: "Define clear permissions" / "Set transparent access rules"

**Protection & Defense** (sounds fearful):

- ❌ "Protect against agents"
- ❌ "Defend your content"
- ❌ "Guard your website"
- ❌ "Prevent AI scraping"
- ✅ **Instead say**: "Share content on your terms" / "Choose how agents engage"

**Blocking & Denial** (sounds negative):

- ❌ "Block unwanted agents"
- ❌ "Stop unauthorized access"
- ❌ "Deny agent requests"
- ❌ "Filter out bad bots"
- ✅ **Instead say**: "Welcome trusted agents" / "Invite compliant agents"

**Problem-Focused** (emphasizes fear):

- ❌ "Prevent agent abuse"
- ❌ "Stop malicious bots"
- ❌ "Combat AI exploitation"
- ❌ "Fight back against scrapers"
- ✅ **Instead say**: "Enable responsible innovation" / "Support trusted agents"

**Adversarial Tone**:

- ❌ "Us vs. them" framing
- ❌ "Websites vs. agents"
- ❌ "Your content, your fortress"
- ✅ **Instead say**: "Websites and agents, together" / "Collaborative web"

---

## 📝 Copy Review Checklist

When reviewing website copy, check each section against these criteria:

### ✅ Does the copy...

1. **Lead with enabling/innovation** rather than control/restriction?
2. **Frame agents as collaborators** rather than threats?
3. **Emphasize clarity and transparency** rather than blocking?
4. **Use the robots.txt analogy** to show this is progressive?
5. **Mention WebMCP/MCP complementarity** to show ecosystem fit?
6. **Focus on win-win outcomes** rather than zero-sum battles?
7. **Highlight flexibility** (opt-in, permissive defaults) rather than mandatory restrictions?
8. **Frame the villain as "legal uncertainty"** not "AI agents"?
9. **Use active, positive language** rather than defensive language?
10. **Show examples of enabling use cases** (research, shopping, booking)?

### ❌ Does the copy...

1. Use words like "control," "block," "prevent," "protect against"?
2. Frame agents as problems to solve rather than innovators to enable?
3. Emphasize what websites can **stop** rather than what agents can **do**?
4. Sound fearful, defensive, or adversarial?
5. Focus on restrictions rather than permissions?
6. Make APoP sound mandatory or heavy-handed?
7. Fail to mention the enabling/innovation angle?
8. Position APoP as competing with (rather than complementing) other protocols?

## 🎨 Tone Guidelines

### Voice Characteristics:

**✅ DO Sound**:

- **Visionary**: "Enabling the future of the web"
- **Collaborative**: "Agents and websites, together"
- **Confident**: "The authorization layer the web needs"
- **Practical**: "Clear rules, less friction, more innovation"
- **Inclusive**: "Both sides win"

**❌ DON'T Sound**:

- **Fearful**: "Protect yourself from agents"
- **Adversarial**: "Fight against AI bots"
- **Paranoid**: "Guard your content"
- **Controlling**: "Take back control"
- **Exclusionary**: "Keep agents out"

---

## 🔍 Specific Section Guidelines

### Homepage Hero:

- **Lead with**: Enabling the agentic web
- **Mention**: robots.txt analogy within first 2 sentences
- **CTA**: "Get Started" or "Create Policy" (not "Block Agents")

### Features Section:

- **Frame features as**: Enabling tools, not blocking tools
- **Example**: "Transparent Access Rules" not "Agent Blocking"
- **Example**: "Policy Builder" not "Restriction Manager"

### For Websites Section:

- **Lead with**: Clarity and choice
- **Example**: "Choose how agents engage with your content"
- **Avoid**: "Protect your content from AI"

### For Agents Section:

- **Lead with**: Legal confidence and clarity
- **Example**: "Build with confidence—know what's allowed"
- **Frame APoP as**: Enabling tool for agents, not burden

### Use Cases:

- **Show**: Positive examples (research, shopping, booking)
- **Avoid**: Only showing "blocking bad agents" examples
- **Balance**: 80% enabling use cases, 20% protection use cases

### Pricing Page:

- **Frame tiers as**: Enabling more capabilities
- **Example**: "Enable 5 websites" not "Protect 5 websites"

### Documentation:

- **Default examples**: Should be permissive (allow: true)
- **Show restrictive examples**: Label as "Premium Content Protection" not "Agent Blocking"

## 🎬 Final Checklist

Before publishing any copy, ask:

- [ ] Does this make me sound like I'm **enabling** innovation?
- [ ] Would an AI agent developer feel **welcomed** or **threatened**?
- [ ] Is the robots.txt analogy **clear**?
- [ ] Am I framing legal uncertainty (not agents) as the **villain**?
- [ ] Does this position APoP as **complementary** to other protocols?
- [ ] Would someone reading this think "this enables the future" or "this blocks progress"?
- [ ] Is my default example policy **permissive** (allow: true)?
- [ ] Am I using **active, positive** language?

**If any answer is uncertain, rewrite toward the enabling/progressive side.**

- ❌ "This will block AI agents from my site"
